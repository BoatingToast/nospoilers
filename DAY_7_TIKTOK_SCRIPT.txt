DAY 7 OF CODING NOSPOILERS

Today, I’m fixing a small dashboard problem that gets more annoying as NoSpoilers grows: there is a lot to explore, but getting from my watchlist to my Movie DNA or community activity takes way too much scrolling.

So I’m building a dashboard section navigator.


CHUNK 1 — DASHBOARD DESTINATIONS

The first chunk maps the five places I visit most: Watchlist, Movie DNA, recommendations, Community, and Favorites.

Each destination gets its own label, icon, and section anchor, so the component stays easy to update as the dashboard changes.


CHUNK 2 — ACTIVE SECTION TRACKING

The second chunk uses an intersection observer to detect which part of the dashboard is on screen.

That means the navigator always highlights where I am without adding scroll listeners or making the page feel heavy.


CHUNK 3 — THE STICKY NAVIGATOR

The final chunk turns those destinations into a polished sticky rail with horizontal scrolling on mobile, clear hover states, and visible keyboard focus.

It stays right below the main navigation, so every section is always one tap away.


LET’S TEST IT

I open the dashboard and the new rail locks into place as I scroll.

I can jump straight to Movie DNA, move down to Community, or head all the way to Favorites. The active pill updates with me, and on mobile I can swipe through every destination without crowding the screen.

Now the NoSpoilers dashboard can keep getting smarter without getting harder to navigate.
